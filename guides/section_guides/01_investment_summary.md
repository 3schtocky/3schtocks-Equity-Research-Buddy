# INVESTMENT SUMMARY

**Purpose:** The scenario page, showing what has to happen for each price target.

**Contents:**
- "PRICE TARGET SCENARIOS" for Bull, Base and Bear. Each gives the price target, the 12–18 month return and a 5–7 sentence paragraph with:
  - Revenue CAGR over the model period, the gross or EBITDA margin path (bps change) and the EPS or net income CAGR if relevant.
  - The operational narrative: which products, segments or macro conditions deliver (or fail to deliver) the numbers.
  - The bear case names specific failure modes. The bull case names specific upside surprises.
- Sidebar facts: Headquarters, CEO, number of employees, GICS sector, GICS sub-industry (from filings or web, sourced).
- Chart (auto): one-year price history extended forward with bear, base and bull target lines ("Last Price, Bear Case, Base Case, Bull Case"). Source: Model.

**Length:** about 1 page. All scenario figures must come from `model.json`.
