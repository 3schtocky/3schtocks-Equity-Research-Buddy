# RISKS TO INVESTMENT THESIS

**Purpose:** Show you have stress-tested the thesis honestly.

**Contents:** 2–4 punny subsections, one risk each:
- A paragraph on the downside mechanism: what could go wrong, how likely, and the quantified exposure (revenue at risk, margin hit, legal or regulatory exposure, leverage).
- Then a bullet beginning with **Mitigant:** (bold) explaining why the risk is manageable or already priced in, with evidence.

**Rules:** Include at least one company-specific risk and one macro or regulatory risk. The bear case in the Investment Summary should echo these risks.
