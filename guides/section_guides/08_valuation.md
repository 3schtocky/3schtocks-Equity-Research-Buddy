# VALUATION ANALYSIS

**Purpose:** Explain how the price targets were derived.

**Contents:**
- **Model Assumptions** (1–2 paragraphs): the base-case revenue CAGR by segment and the drivers behind it, the margin trajectory (gross, EBITDA and opex lines as % of revenue), the CapEx path, WACC inputs (risk-free rate, beta, ERP, cost of debt, capital weights), the terminal method (exit multiple vs perpetuity) and why it fits this company, and the weighting across DCF, forward P/E and EV/EBITDA.
- Bull and bear deltas: what changes in each scenario.
- Exhibits (auto): DCF output for Base, Bear and Bull; a sensitivity table (WACC vs exit multiple); a football field; and a Sum of the Parts table when segments differ materially.

**Rule:** Every number here is read from `model.json` and `assumptions.yaml`. Prose explains the judgment behind the assumptions.
