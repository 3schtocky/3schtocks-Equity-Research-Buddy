# BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Purpose:** How the company makes money, segment by segment.

**Contents:**
- Opening paragraph: identity, scale, founding year and founder, major rebrands or M&A, business model in one line, reporting segments with % of last FY revenue.
- One bold subsection per segment: "**Segment Name (xx.x% of FY'24 Revenue)**". For each: products, customers, pricing mechanism, key KPIs (units, users, volumes, price and mix), recent growth, notable launches.
- For platforms or multiple product lines, a bolded run-in for each major product ("**Instagram:** ...").
- Chart (auto): segment revenue mix pie or donut for the last FY. Optionally a geographic mix.

**Sources:** Primarily 10-K Item 1 and segment note, plus recent 10-Q and earnings materials. Every % of revenue ties to XBRL segment data.
