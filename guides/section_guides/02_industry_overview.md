# INDUSTRY OVERVIEW

**Purpose:** Show you understand the industry better than a generalist, and set up the thesis.

**Structure:** 4–7 punny subsections, 1–2 paragraphs each. Typical arc:
1. Market size and growth (TAM, historical and forecast CAGR, mix shifts) with a chart.
2. Recent cycle: what slowed or accelerated the industry and why (macro, regulation, technology).
3. 2–4 structural trends that matter for THIS company's thesis (e.g. AI adoption, pricing, consolidation, policy).
4. Competitive dynamics and market share (a share chart when possible).
5. Adjacent or emerging opportunities the company is positioned for.

**Rules:** Industry data should be recent (CY'24/CY'25 or later) from named sources (industry bodies, government data, research firms, company filings). One or two charts with Source lines. Mention the covered company by ticker where the trend touches it, but keep the focus on the industry.
**Placement:** Some reports put Business Overview first. Choose the order that makes the story clearer. Default: Industry, then Business.
