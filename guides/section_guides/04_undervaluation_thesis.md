# UNDERVALUATION & THESIS

**Purpose:** Why the stock is mispriced now. This is the heart of the report.

**Contents:**
- Opening paragraph: the current NTM P/E (or the sector-appropriate multiple: EV/EBITDA, P/FFO for REITs, P/TBV for banks) against its 1-, 2- and 3-year median (all from market data). Then the macro and sector context, and why now is the time to initiate.
- 2–4 punny subsections that tell the story of the multiple: a timeline of earnings beats and misses, guidance changes, competitor or regulatory events and macro shocks, each with the dated stock or multiple reaction ("shares rose ~20.0% in the two weeks following ...").
- The contrarian point: what the Street is focused on, and why that focus is short-sighted or wrong.
- Exhibits (auto): "<TICKER> One-Year NTM P/E", "Two-Year NTM P/E" and "Three-Year NTM P/E" (or EV/EBITDA) with the median line. Source: market data.

**Rule:** No "Broader Portfolio Fit" subsection (we are not a fund). If sector positioning matters, fold it into the thesis.
