# PEER GROUP ANALYSIS

**Purpose:** Establish the comparison set for valuation.

**Contents:**
- 4–6 peers, each with a 4–6 sentence profile: full name (TICKER), HQ, what it does, founding, reporting segments with % of last FY revenue, and how it competes with or resembles the covered company.
- One-year and YTD stock performance for each peer (auto).
- **Comps table** (auto, from `erb model`): Market Cap, EV, Sales growth (LTM, NTM), EPS growth, EBITDA margin, profit margin, EV/Sales, EV/EBITDA, P/E (LTM, NTM), with High, Mean, Median and Low rows. A second table covers returns (ROIC, ROE, ROA), leverage (Debt/EBITDA, Debt/Equity), coverage, liquidity and credit rating.
- Where the company spans two industries, use two peer groups (e.g. "California Utilities Peer Group" and "Energy Infrastructure Peer Group").
