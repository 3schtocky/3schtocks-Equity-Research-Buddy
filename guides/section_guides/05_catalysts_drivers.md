# CATALYSTS & DRIVERS

**Purpose:** What closes the valuation gap, and when.

**Contents:**
- Opening paragraph: the company's long-term value-creation logic, and what the Street is missing.
- 3–6 punny subsections, one catalyst each. Each must have:
  1. **Mechanism:** a product launch, capacity coming online, pricing action, M&A, margin program, regulatory decision or buyback.
  2. **Quantification:** revenue, margin, EPS or KPI impact (from filings, guidance or the model; sensitivity numbers come from `model.json`).
  3. **Timing:** a dated milestone within or near the 12–18 month horizon.
- A supporting chart where a catalyst has a forecastable series.
